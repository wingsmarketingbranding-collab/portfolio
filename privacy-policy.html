Privacy Policy

Your privacy and personal information matter.

Last Updated: 24-Nov-2026

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you access our website.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with our website, information collected may include:

  • Name and email address voluntarily provided through communications.
  • Browser type, device information and IP address.
  • Website usage and interaction information.
  • Information provided when submitting enquiries.

2. Use of Information

Information may be used to improve website functionality, respond to enquiries, maintain security, understand visitor preferences and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and analytics technologies to understand visitor activity and improve the browsing experience.

Where legally required, consent will be obtained before activating non-essential tracking technologies. Users can manage cookie preferences through their browser settings.

4. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites. These platforms operate under their respective privacy policies and terms of service.

Users should review the privacy practices of external platforms before providing personal information.

5. Data Protection

We take reasonable measures to protect information under our control against unauthorized access, disclosure and misuse. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and User Rights

Personal information is retained only as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected or as required by applicable law.

Subject to applicable legislation, users may request access, correction or deletion of their personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.
