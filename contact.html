1. General Enquiries

For questions regarding website content, accessibility, navigation or general information, please contact our website support team.

When submitting an enquiry, please include a clear description of your concern and relevant information that may help us understand the issue.

2. Technical Assistance

If you experience technical difficulties while browsing our website, please provide details about the issue, including the device, browser and affected page.

Screenshots may be included where necessary, provided they do not contain confidential or sensitive personal information.

3. Third-Party Platform Support

For account-specific enquiries or technical issues relating to an external platform, users should contact the respective platform's verified support service.

External platforms operate under their own policies, terms and customer support procedures. Our website cannot resolve account-related matters on behalf of an independent service provider unless expressly authorized.

Any commercial or referral relationship must be accurately disclosed, and external services must comply with applicable laws.

4. Privacy and Grievance Support

For concerns regarding personal information, data processing, privacy rights or requests for correction or deletion of personal information, please contact the designated privacy or grievance representative.

You can also review our Privacy Policy for further information about our data handling practices.

5. Feedback and Suggestions

We value constructive feedback regarding website functionality, design, accessibility and content quality.

Your suggestions can help us identify improvements and provide a better browsing experience.

Enquiries will be reviewed based on their nature, complexity and available support resources.

Response times may vary depending on support availability and the information required to address the concern.
