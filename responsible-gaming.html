Responsible Gaming and Legal Disclosures

Stay informed. Play responsibly. Prioritize your wellbeing.

Last Updated: 24-Nov-2026

Gaming and digital entertainment should be approached responsibly, with consideration for personal wellbeing, privacy, financial safety and applicable legal requirements.

This page provides important information about healthy gaming habits, eligibility requirements, external platforms and legal responsibilities.

Manage Your Time

Set reasonable gaming limits and maintain a healthy balance with your everyday responsibilities.

Prioritize Wellbeing

Take regular breaks and prioritize your physical health, mental wellbeing and relationships.

Stay Informed

Understand applicable age limits, platform requirements and legal restrictions before participating.

1. Responsible Gaming Principles

Digital entertainment should remain a recreational activity. It should not interfere with education, employment, personal health, relationships or everyday responsibilities.

Users are encouraged to make informed decisions and maintain control over their participation in gaming-related activities.

2. Healthy Gaming Habits

We encourage visitors to adopt the following responsible gaming practices:

  • Set reasonable limits on gaming time.
  • Take regular breaks during extended sessions.
  • Maintain a healthy sleep schedule.
  • Balance gaming with work, education, exercise and social activities.
  • Avoid gaming when experiencing emotional distress or difficulty maintaining control.
  • Seek appropriate support if gaming begins to negatively affect everyday life.

3. Age Restrictions and Eligibility

Users must comply with the applicable minimum age requirements and eligibility conditions associated with any digital platform or service they access.

Gambling and other age-restricted activities must not be accessed by minors or individuals who do not meet the applicable legal age requirements.

Meeting an age requirement does not make an otherwise prohibited activity lawful.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to monitor children's digital activities and use suitable parental control features.

4. Financial Awareness and Gambling Risks

Gaming should not be treated as a source of income or a solution to financial difficulties.

Gambling and activities involving financial stakes carry a risk of financial loss and may contribute to compulsive behavior, emotional distress and financial hardship.

Users should never borrow money to gamble or attempt to recover losses through continued participation.

Activities involving financial stakes may also be prohibited by applicable law.

5. Legal and Geographical Restrictions

Access to digital services is subject to applicable laws, regulations and geographical restrictions.

Activities prohibited in a user's country, state or jurisdiction must not be accessed, advertised, promoted or facilitated.

In India, applicable restrictions on online money games, betting, gambling and related advertising must be observed. External registration links must not be used to facilitate prohibited activities.

Important Legal Notice

The availability of a website or external platform does not establish its legality, licensing status or regulatory authorization. A disclaimer, age notice or intermediary website does not override applicable laws.

6. Third-Party Platforms and External Services

External platforms operate under their own terms, privacy policies, eligibility requirements and regulatory obligations.

Where external platforms provide betting, gambling or other financially restricted services, the nature of those services must be clearly identified and must not be misrepresented as ordinary video gaming.

References to external services do not constitute verification of their licensing, security, legality or regulatory authorization.

7. Transparency and Informed Participation

Transparency and informed decision-making are important aspects of responsible digital participation. Visitors are encouraged to understand the nature, features, eligibility requirements and applicable terms of any external service before engaging with it.

External platforms operate under their respective policies, terms of service and regulatory requirements. References to third-party services do not constitute a guarantee of their safety, licensing status or availability in any particular region.

Any applicable commercial, promotional or referral relationships must be clearly disclosed alongside the relevant content. Users should independently evaluate external services and ensure that their participation complies with applicable laws.

For additional information or clarification regarding our website and its external relationships, please visit our Contact Information page.

8. Advertising and Platform Compliance

Advertising and promotional activities must comply with applicable laws and the requirements of the relevant advertising platforms.

Gambling-related advertising may require specific authorization or may be prohibited depending on the service and target jurisdiction.

This website must not be used to conceal restricted services, misrepresent destination links or circumvent advertising platform review processes.

The presence of these legal disclosures does not constitute approval, certification or endorsement by Facebook, Instagram, Meta or any regulatory authority.

9. Privacy and Digital Security

Users should exercise caution when sharing personal information online and independently verify the authenticity of websites and support representatives.

  • Never share passwords or one-time passwords with unauthorized individuals.
  • Protect banking credentials and confidential personal information.
  • Review the privacy practices of external platforms.
  • Report suspicious communications or unauthorized activity.

For additional information, please review our Privacy Policy .

10. Recognizing Problematic Gaming Behavior

Users should consider reducing or stopping participation if gaming or gambling begins to negatively affect their wellbeing.

Potential warning signs include:

  • Difficulty controlling participation or time spent gaming.
  • Neglecting personal or professional responsibilities.
  • Experiencing emotional distress related to gaming activities.
  • Spending beyond personal financial limits.
  • Continuing participation despite harmful consequences.

11. Help and Support

If gaming or gambling negatively affects your mental health, financial circumstances or everyday life, consider stopping participation and seeking assistance.

Speak with a trusted individual, qualified healthcare professional or appropriate addiction support service.

Where available, consider using legitimate self-exclusion tools, spending restrictions and account access controls.

For website-related concerns, please visit our Contact Information page.

Third-party game titles, trademarks, logos, characters and brand assets belong to their respective owners.

Illustrative and AI-generated gaming artwork does not represent official gameplay or establish affiliation with a game developer or publisher.

13. Updates to These Disclosures

This page may be updated periodically to reflect changes in applicable laws, regulatory requirements, website functionality or commercial relationships.

Users are encouraged to review the latest version of this page alongside our Terms and Conditions and Disclaimer .

Stay Informed. Play Responsibly.

Personal wellbeing, informed decisions, transparency and legal compliance should always come first when engaging with digital entertainment.
